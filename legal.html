How we handle complaints

If something has gone wrong, tell us first. Call 1300 745 837 or email orders@silverlinestackle.com.au with your order number. We acknowledge every complaint within one business day and aim to resolve it within ten business days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can take the matter further:

  1. Consumer affairs in your state or territory — NSW Fair Trading, Consumer Affairs Victoria, Queensland Office of Fair Trading, Consumer and Business Services SA, Consumer Protection WA, Consumer Building and Occupational Services Tasmania, NT Consumer Affairs, or Access Canberra.
  2. The Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC) for consumer law matters.
  3. The Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) for privacy complaints, on 1300 363 992 or at oaic.gov.au.
  4. Your state fisheries authority for questions about fishing regulations, licences and bag limits.

Where we stand. Silver Lines Tackle Co. trades on Bundjalung country on the Clarence River. We pay our respects to the Bundjalung people, their Elders past and present, and acknowledge that they have cared for these waters and fisheries for tens of thousands of years. We encourage all customers to fish within state bag and size limits and to follow the rules set by your local fisheries authority.

Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST. Fishing regulations vary between states and territories — it is your responsibility to hold a current recreational fishing licence where required.
