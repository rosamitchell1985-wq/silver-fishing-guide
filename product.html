Delivery and returns

Free standard delivery on orders over $99. Express is $21.95 and click & collect from our Yamba store is free. Every order is covered by a 30-day return window and by the Australian Consumer Law. Read the Shipping & Delivery Policy and the Returns & Refunds Policy.

Warranty

All gear carries a minimum 12-month manufacturer warranty, and our own stated warranties run from 2 to 5 years depending on the item. This is in addition to, and does not replace, your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. See the Warranty Policy.

Where we stand. Silver Lines Tackle Co. trades on Bundjalung country on the Clarence River. We pay our respects to the Bundjalung people, their Elders past and present, and acknowledge that they have cared for these waters and fisheries for tens of thousands of years. We encourage all customers to fish within state bag and size limits and to follow the rules set by your local fisheries authority.

Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST. Fishing regulations vary between states and territories — it is your responsibility to hold a current recreational fishing licence where required.
