What happens next

  1. You will receive a confirmation email within a few minutes, with your order number and an itemised tax invoice. If it has not arrived in 30 minutes, check your spam folder and then call us.
  2. We pick and pack your order. Orders placed before 1pm on a business day are usually packed the same day. We check every rod and reel before it leaves the shop.
  3. You get a tracking number. Australia Post emails it to you when the parcel is lodged. Click & collect orders get a "ready for pickup" email with our opening hours.
  4. Something wrong? Tell us. Damaged, missing or not as described — call 1300 745 837 or email orders@silverlinestackle.com.au within 14 days. We fix it, and under the Australian Consumer Law you may be entitled to a refund or replacement.
Keep this page or your email as your proof of purchase. It is also useful for warranty claims — see the Warranty Policy. Under Australian Consumer Law, you should keep your proof of purchase, and a bank or card statement showing the transaction is usually enough if the email is lost.

Where we stand. Silver Lines Tackle Co. trades on Bundjalung country on the Clarence River. We pay our respects to the Bundjalung people, their Elders past and present, and acknowledge that they have cared for these waters and fisheries for tens of thousands of years. We encourage all customers to fish within state bag and size limits and to follow the rules set by your local fisheries authority.

Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST. Fishing regulations vary between states and territories — it is your responsibility to hold a current recreational fishing licence where required.
