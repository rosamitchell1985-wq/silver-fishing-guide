Common questions, answered honestly

Do I need a fishing licence in Australia?

It depends on your state. New South Wales, Victoria, South Australia, Tasmania, the ACT and the Northern Territory require a recreational fishing licence in most freshwater and some saltwater situations, while Queensland and Western Australia generally do not for basic recreational line fishing. Rules differ for boats, freshwater and some species. Always check with your state fisheries authority, and note that every dollar from a NSW licence goes back into fish stocking and habitat.

Is delivery really free over $99?

Yes — after any discount is applied, standard Australia Post parcel delivery with tracking is free on orders of $99 and over. Below that it is a flat $12.95. Express is $21.95, and click & collect from our Yamba store is always free.

Can I return something if it does not suit?

Yes. You have 30 days from delivery to return unused items in their original condition for a refund or exchange, and you also have your rights under the Australian Consumer Law, which sit on top of that and cannot be excluded. See our Returns & Refunds Policy.
