Shipping & Delivery Policy

Delivery methods, timeframes and costs, the free delivery threshold, tracking, click & collect, and what we do if a parcel is lost, delayed or damaged.

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Silver Lines Tackle Co. · ABN 48 271 903 556

1. Where we deliver

We deliver to Australian addresses only. This includes residential and business addresses, PO boxes and Australia Post parcel lockers. We do not currently ship to overseas addresses, and we do not ship to addresses outside Australia.

We cannot deliver to an unattended address without your instruction. If you ask us to leave a parcel unattended (a “safe drop”), risk in the parcel passes to you at the moment it is left, although your rights under the Australian Consumer Law are unaffected.

Dispatch cut-off is 1pm on a business day. Orders placed after 1pm, on a weekend or on a public holiday are packed on the next business day.

3. Regional, rural and remote addresses

Australia is a large country and delivery to regional, rural and remote areas can take longer than the timeframes above. Western Australia, the Northern Territory, far north Queensland, and island and remote communities are the most affected. If you would like an honest estimate for your postcode before you order, call us on 1300 745 837 and we will tell you what we usually see.

4. Tracking your order

Every parcel is lodged with Australia Post and carries a tracking number. Australia Post emails the tracking number to the address you gave us when the parcel is lodged. Click & collect orders receive a “ready for collection” email with our opening hours.

If you have not received a tracking email within two business days of ordering, check your spam folder and then call us on 1300 745 837. We will chase it up with the carrier on your behalf.

5. Oversized and bulky items

Chairs, trolleys and long rods are sent in a rigid tube or a large carton. If your order attracts a bulky-item surcharge, we will contact you before dispatch to confirm the amount and obtain your approval. We will never add a charge after payment without asking you first.

6. Click & collect

Click & collect is free. We will email you when your order is packed and ready, usually the next business day after ordering. Please bring photo ID and your order number. If someone else is collecting on your behalf, add their name in the delivery notes when you order, or call us before they arrive.

We hold click & collect orders for 14 days. If you need longer, call us and we will hold it for up to 30 days. After that we will contact you to arrange delivery or a refund.

7. If you are not home

Australia Post will generally leave a card and take the parcel to the nearest post office or parcel locker if no one is available to receive it. You then have a limited period to collect it, usually five business days for a post office. If the parcel is returned to us uncollected, we will contact you to arrange redelivery. A redelivery charge equal to the original delivery charge may apply, and we will tell you the amount before we proceed. If you would prefer a refund instead, we will refund the price of the goods, less the original delivery charge.

8. Lost, delayed or damaged parcels

We are responsible for getting your goods to you safely. If a parcel is lost or damaged in transit:

  • Call us on 1300 745 837 or email orders@silverlinestackle.com.au as soon as you notice.
  • For damage, keep the packaging, the contents and the delivery note exactly as they arrived, and take photographs if you can. This helps us resolve the claim with the carrier quickly.
  • We will investigate with Australia Post, which usually takes 3–10 business days, and will then arrange a replacement or a refund as you prefer.

Under the Australian Consumer Law you may be entitled to choose the remedy where there has been a major failure. You do not have to accept a repair or a replacement where a refund is your right. See our Returns & Refunds Policy.

9. Incorrect address provided

Please check your delivery address carefully at checkout. If a parcel cannot be delivered because the address you gave us was incorrect or incomplete, and it is returned to us, we will contact you to arrange redelivery. A redelivery charge may apply. We will always tell you the cost before charging it, and you may choose a refund instead.

10. Stock availability and backorders

We keep stock levels current on this website, but occasionally two customers buy the last item at the same time. If an item is unavailable after you order, we will contact you within one business day and offer you the choice of a full refund, a substitute item, or to wait for the item to be restocked. We will not simply hold your money and hope you forget.

11. Questions about delivery

Call 1300 745 837, Monday to Friday 9am–5pm and Saturday 9am–4pm (Australian Eastern time), or email orders@silverlinestackle.com.au. Have your order number ready and we will be able to help straight away.

Questions about this policy? Contact us on 1300 745 837 or at orders@silverlinestackle.com.au, or write to Silver Lines Tackle Co., PO Box 212, Yamba NSW 2464.
