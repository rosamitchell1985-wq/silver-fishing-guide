Website Disclaimer

The limits of the general information on this site, your responsibility for fishing regulations and safety on the water, and how far our liability extends.

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Silver Lines Tackle Co. · ABN 48 271 903 556

1. General information only

The content on this website — including our free fishing guides, product descriptions, safety information and answers to frequently asked questions — is provided as general information for Australian recreational anglers. It is not professional, legal, medical or safety advice, and it is not tailored to your individual circumstances, abilities or health.

Before relying on anything you read here, consider whether it suits your own situation. If you have a health condition, a disability, or you take medication that affects your balance, judgement or strength, speak to your doctor before you go fishing, particularly if you plan to fish near rocks, breakwalls or deep water.

2. Fishing regulations are your responsibility

Recreational fishing in Australia is regulated by each state and territory. Rules change, and they differ between jurisdictions, between freshwater and saltwater, and between species.

We do not warrant that any statement on this website about licences, bag limits, size limits, closed seasons, protected species or permitted gear is current or correct for your location. It is your responsibility to:

  • hold a current recreational fishing licence or permit where your state or territory requires one;
  • know and observe the bag limits, size limits and closed seasons that apply where you fish;
  • observe rules about protected species, marine parks and sanctuary zones; and
  • check the current rules with your state fisheries authority before every trip.

Nothing on this website should be read as encouraging or condoning any breach of fishing regulations. Where this website describes a product or technique, we assume you will use it lawfully.

3. Safety is your responsibility

Fishing involves real risks, including drowning, slips and falls, sun exposure, heat stress, cuts from hooks and knives, and marine hazards. We are a retailer. We are not your safety adviser, and our products cannot remove these risks.

You are responsible for:

  • assessing conditions, including weather, tide, swell and visibility, before you set out;
  • choosing safe ground to stand on and never turning your back on the ocean;
  • wearing an appropriate personal flotation device whenever there is a risk of entering the water;
  • servicing inflatable life jackets annually and checking them before every trip;
  • the correct use, maintenance and care of anything you buy from us; and
  • telling someone where you are going and when you expect to return.

In an emergency, call Triple Zero (000), or 112 from a mobile if your own network has no signal.

4. Product images, colours and specifications

We photograph our own products and describe them as accurately as we can. Even so:

  • Colours can appear differently on different screens, and we cannot guarantee an exact match between a photograph and the physical product.
  • Photographs may show tackle, lures, line or accessories that are not included in the box. What is included is stated in the “What is in the box” section of each product page.
  • Manufacturers occasionally change specifications, materials or finishes without notice. We update product information when we become aware of a change, but a minor specification change by a manufacturer does not of itself mean the product is faulty.
  • Weight, length and capacity figures are nominal. Very small variations in manufactured goods are normal.

If a product you receive is materially different from its description, that may be a major failure under the Australian Consumer Law, and you may be entitled to a refund or replacement. See our Returns & Refunds Policy.

5. Stock availability and pricing errors

Product listings and stock levels are kept as current as we can, but errors occur. If an item is out of stock, or a price is listed incorrectly because of a typographical or system error, we will contact you before dispatching and offer the corrected price, a substitute, or a full refund. A price shown in error does not bind us to the sale, but we will never take your money and then refuse to talk to you about it.

This website may link to websites we do not control, including government fisheries authorities, the Bureau of Meteorology and product manufacturer sites. Those links are provided for convenience only. We do not endorse, and are not responsible for, the content, accuracy, availability or privacy practices of any external website. When you follow a link to another site, that site's own terms and privacy policy apply.

We do not warrant that this website will be available at all times, uninterrupted or free of errors. Our Terms & Conditions set out the limits of our liability for website unavailability.
