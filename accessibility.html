Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to WCAG 2.2 Level AA, the accessibility features built into this website, known limitations, and how to get help or buy by phone.

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Silver Lines Tackle Co. · ABN 48 271 903 556

1. Our commitment

Silver Lines Tackle Co. wants everyone to be able to browse and shop here, including people with low vision, limited hand strength, hearing loss, cognitive differences and people who use screen readers, voice control or magnification software.

This site has been built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.2 at Level AA, published by the W3C. We treat that standard as our minimum, not our ceiling, and we test the site with keyboard-only navigation, increased text sizes and a screen reader.

2. Accessibility features built into this site

  • Text size controls at the top of every page — three settings (A, A+, A++) that enlarge the whole site, not just some of it.
  • High contrast mode that removes gradient backgrounds and low-contrast greys, and strengthens borders, for people with low vision or photophobia.
  • Large base text of 18 pixels, with line height of 1.7 and generous paragraph spacing.
  • Large touch and click targets — buttons and links are at least 44 by 44 pixels, and the quantity buttons in the cart are 48 pixels.
  • Visible focus indicators — a thick amber outline shows exactly where you are when navigating by keyboard or switch.
  • Skip to main content link as the first focusable item on every page.
  • Text alternatives for all meaningful images, and empty alternatives for decorative ones.
  • Semantic headings and landmarks so screen reader users can jump between regions and heading levels in a logical order.
  • Form labels and error messages that are programmatically associated with their fields, with errors written as plain instructions rather than codes.
  • Live regions that announce when an item is added to the cart and when a page region changes, without moving your focus unexpectedly.
  • No reliance on colour alone — statuses are conveyed with words and symbols as well as colour.
  • No automatic movement, autoplay or carousels, and animation is suppressed entirely if your device requests reduced motion.
  • Zoom and reflow — content reflows to a single column so it can be read at 320 pixels wide and zoomed to 200% without horizontal scrolling.
  • Print-friendly — receipts and guides print cleanly without navigation or advertising.

3. Keyboard shortcuts and navigation

Everything on this site can be done with a keyboard. Press Tab to move forward and Shift+Tab to move back, Enter or Space to activate a button, and the down arrow to open a question in the FAQ. There are no keyboard traps: you can always Tab out of any element.

4. Conformance status and testing

We assess the site against WCAG 2.2 Level AA. At the date of this statement our own testing indicates:

  • Perceivable, Operable and Understandable: no known failures.
  • Robust: no known failures in the tested screen reader and browser combinations — NVDA with Firefox and Chrome on Windows, VoiceOver with Safari on macOS and iOS, and TalkBack with Chrome on Android.

We re-test the site when we change the design and at least once every 12 months. We do not claim perfect conformance, and we welcome reports of problems.

5. Known limitations

Being honest matters more than a clean claim, so here is what we know is imperfect:

  • Product photography shows hooks, lures, sinkers and similar tackle in detail. Where a photograph conveys information that cannot be conveyed in text, we describe it in the surrounding text and in the product specifications table.
  • The payment form includes validation on card number, expiry and security code. Errors are announced and described in words, but some browsers announce the expiry prompt as “MM slash YY”, which is not ideal for everyone. If it causes you trouble, please call us and we will take payment over the phone.
  • Third-party payment pages (for example a PayPal checkout) are controlled by the payment provider, and we cannot guarantee their accessibility. We provide telephone ordering as an equivalent alternative for every product.
  • Older browsers such as Internet Explorer are not supported. The site works on current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, and on mobile devices.

6. Alternative ways to buy

If any part of this website is difficult for you, we offer full alternatives at no difference in price:

  • Telephone ordering on 1300 745 837, Monday to Friday 9am–5pm and Saturday 9am–4pm (AEST). We will take your order, apply your Seniors Card discount and post it out the same day.
  • Large print — we will post or email a large-print product list and our guides on request.
  • In-store service at Shop 4, 118 River Esplanade, Yamba NSW 2464 — step-free entry, seating available, and we will set up your gear for you.
  • Email to orders@silverlinestackle.com.au — tell us what you need and we will reply with a plain-text quote.

7. Feedback and contact

If you find an accessibility barrier on this site, please tell us. We want specifics, because that is what lets us fix it.

  • Phone: 1300 745 837
  • Email: orders@silverlinestackle.com.au with the subject line “Accessibility”
  • Post: Silver Lines Tackle Co., PO Box 212, Yamba NSW 2464

We acknowledge accessibility feedback within two business days and aim to fix genuine barriers within 30 days, or to provide a workable alternative immediately where a fix will take longer.

8. Formal complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response to an accessibility matter, you can contact the Australian Human Rights Commission on 1300 656 419 or at humanrights.gov.au, or the relevant anti-discrimination or equal opportunity body in your state or territory. We will cooperate fully with any such process.

Questions about this policy? Contact us on 1300 745 837 or at orders@silverlinestackle.com.au, or write to Silver Lines Tackle Co., PO Box 212, Yamba NSW 2464.
