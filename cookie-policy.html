Cookie Policy

Every piece of browser storage this website uses, what it is for, how long it lasts, and how to clear it or block it entirely.

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Silver Lines Tackle Co. · ABN 48 271 903 556

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how the Silver Lines Tackle Co. website uses cookies, local storage and similar technologies. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

Cookies are small text files a website asks your browser to store. Local storage is a similar browser feature that stores data on your device without transmitting it with every request. We use both sparingly, and we do not use advertising cookies.

2. What we store and why

This website uses browser storage for exactly four things, all of which are necessary for the site to work or for your own convenience:

NameTypePurposeRetention
sl_cart_v1Local storageRemembers the items in your shopping cart so they survive when you move between pagesUntil you clear it or complete an order
sl_buyer_v1Local storageSaves your name, delivery address, email and phone so you do not have to retype them next timeUntil you clear it; we do not store card details
sl_prefs_v1Local storageRemembers your text-size and high-contrast choicesUntil you clear it
sl_order_v1Local storageHolds your completed order so the payment-success page and receipt can be displayedUntil you clear it or place another order

We do not use third-party advertising cookies, we do not build cross-site profiles of your browsing, and we do not sell data derived from cookies to anyone. Our payment processor may set its own strictly necessary cookies during checkout; those are governed by the processor's own privacy policy.

3. Changes to your cart and order data

You can clear all of it at any time by clearing your browser's site data and cookies for this website. Instructions for the major browsers are below. Clearing storage will empty your cart and reset your accessibility preferences; it will not affect any order you have already placed, and it will not delete your order history from our own records.

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies → See all site data and permissions.
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Manage Data.

4. Blocking storage entirely

If you block cookies and site data completely, the shop will not work: you will not be able to add items to a cart or complete an order online. Everything else — the guides, product information and policies — will still work normally. If you block storage and still want to buy, please call us on 1300 745 837 and we will take your order over the phone.

5. Do Not Track and analytics

We respect the Do Not Track browser signal and do not track you across other websites. We do not run advertising pixels, remarketing tags or social media trackers on this website. Any website usage statistics we review are aggregated and do not identify you.

6. Changes to this policy

If we add a new cookie, we will update this table and the “last updated” date before the change takes effect. We will not introduce advertising or tracking cookies without telling you clearly and seeking your consent where the law requires it.

Questions about this policy? Contact us on 1300 745 837 or at orders@silverlinestackle.com.au, or write to Silver Lines Tackle Co., PO Box 212, Yamba NSW 2464.
