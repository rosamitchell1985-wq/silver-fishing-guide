Frequently asked questions

Twelve questions we are asked most often, answered properly. If yours is not here, call 1300 745 837 or send us a message.

Do I need a recreational fishing licence in Australia?

It depends on your state or territory, and it can depend on whether you are fishing freshwater or saltwater. New South Wales requires a licence for most recreational fishing, and the fee goes into fish stocking and habitat. Victoria, South Australia, Tasmania, the ACT and the Northern Territory also have licence or permit requirements in many situations. Queensland and Western Australia generally do not require a licence for basic recreational line fishing, but bag and size limits apply everywhere. Always confirm with your state fisheries authority before you go — rules change, and penalties are real.

Is delivery really free over $99?

Yes. After any Seniors Card discount is applied, if your order totals $99 or more, standard Australia Post parcel delivery with tracking is free. Below $99 it is a flat $12.95. Express Post is $21.95 and click & collect from our Yamba store is always free.

How long will my order take to arrive?

Standard delivery is 3–6 business days to most Australian addresses, and Express Post is 1–2 business days to metropolitan areas. Remote and regional addresses can take longer, especially in Western Australia and the Northern Territory. We lodge orders placed before 1pm on a business day the same day. Full details are in the Shipping & Delivery Policy.

Can I return something if it does not suit me?

Yes. You have 30 days from delivery to return unused items in their original packaging for a refund or exchange, provided you have proof of purchase. That is our own policy, and it sits alongside your rights under the Australian Consumer Law, which cannot be excluded. If an item is faulty, significantly different from its description or does not do what it is supposed to do, you may be entitled to a repair, replacement or refund regardless of the 30 days. See the Returns & Refunds Policy.

Do you offer a discount for Seniors Card holders?

Yes — 10% off the full order, including sale items, for holders of any Australian state Seniors Card or the Commonwealth Seniors Health Card. Tick the box at checkout, or mention it if you order by phone. On a first order or on collection we may ask to see the card. The full terms are in the Seniors Discount Terms.

Can I order by phone or in person?

Absolutely, and plenty of our customers do. Call 1300 745 837 between 9am and 5pm Monday to Friday, or 9am to 4pm Saturday (AEST). We will take your order, apply your discount and post it out the same day. You can also visit Shop 4, 118 River Esplanade, Yamba NSW 2464 and we will set the gear up for you before you leave, at no charge.

Do you set up rods and reels before shipping?

Yes. Every rod and reel combo is spooled, the drag is set and a leader is tied on before it is packed. You can fish it the day it arrives. If you would rather set it up yourself, tell us in the delivery notes and we will send it unspooled.

Are your life jackets approved for use in Australia?

Our inflatable life jackets are certified to Australian Standard AS 4758 and carry the PFD 1 (Level 100) marking. They come with a service record card and a whistle. Inflatable jackets must be serviced annually and the cylinder checked before each trip. We also stock foam PFDs for anyone who would rather not maintain an inflatable.

What payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard and American Express, PayPal, and bank transfer by invoice for orders over $200. In store we also accept EFTPOS and cash. We do not store your full card number on this website — only the last four digits, so we can match a payment to an order.

Is your website accessible if I have low vision or use a screen reader?

We have built it to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines version 2.2 at Level AA. There are text size buttons (A, A+, A++) and a high-contrast mode at the top of every page, all text can be enlarged to 200% without loss of function, and every image has a text alternative. If you hit a barrier, call us and we will take your order over the phone. Read the Accessibility Statement.

Do you ship outside Australia?

Not currently. We deliver to Australian addresses only, including PO boxes and parcel lockers through Australia Post. If you are an Australian customer temporarily overseas, we can ship to an Australian address for you to collect on your return.

What should I do if my parcel arrives damaged?

Call us on 1300 745 837 or email orders@silverlinestackle.com.au within 14 days, and keep the packaging and the item as it arrived. Photographs help us resolve it quickly with the carrier. We will arrange a replacement or a refund — and under the Australian Consumer Law you may be entitled to choose the remedy for a major failure. You do not have to accept a repair for a major problem.

Still unsure? We would rather spend ten minutes on the phone with you than have you buy the wrong thing. Call 1300 745 837, Monday to Friday 9am–5pm and Saturday 9am–4pm (AEST).
