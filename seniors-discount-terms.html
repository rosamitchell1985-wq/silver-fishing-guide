Seniors Discount Terms

Who qualifies for the 10% Seniors Card discount, which cards we accept, how to claim and verify it, and what it can and cannot be combined with.

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Silver Lines Tackle Co. · ABN 48 271 903 556

1. The discount

Silver Lines Tackle Co. offers a 10% discount off the total order value to holders of an eligible Australian Seniors Card. The discount is applied to the goods subtotal before delivery charges are calculated.

2. Who is eligible

The discount is available to holders of any of the following, in their own name:

  • A Seniors Card issued by any Australian state or territory (NSW, VIC, QLD, SA, WA, TAS, NT or ACT);
  • A Commonwealth Seniors Health Card issued by Services Australia;
  • A state Seniors Business Discount Card or equivalent state-issued seniors card.

You must be the cardholder. The discount is not transferable and cannot be applied on behalf of another person, including a family member making a purchase for you. If a family member is ordering on your behalf, they may give your name and card details over the phone and we will apply the discount to that order.

3. How to claim it

  • Online: tick the “I hold an Australian Seniors Card” box in your cart or at checkout. The 10% is deducted immediately and shown as a separate line in your order summary.
  • By phone: mention your Seniors Card when you call 1300 745 837 and we will apply it before taking payment.
  • In store: show your card at the counter.

4. Verification

Ticking the box online is an honour system, because we would rather make it easy for you than interrogate you for the sake of a few dollars. We may ask to see your card:

  • on a first order, if the order value is unusually high;
  • on collection of a click & collect order; or
  • where we reasonably suspect the discount is being used by someone who is not eligible.

If a discount is applied and we later confirm that the cardholder is not eligible, we may invoice you for the discounted amount. We will always contact you first and give you the chance to provide proof of eligibility.

5. What it applies to

The discount applies to all stocked products, including sale items and bundles. It does not apply to:

  • delivery charges, which are calculated after the discount;
  • gift vouchers; and
  • existing business trade accounts, which are priced separately.

6. Combining with other offers

The Seniors Card discount can be combined with free standard delivery on orders over $99. It cannot be combined with other promotional discount codes or staff pricing unless we say so in writing for a specific promotion. Where two discounts could apply, we will automatically give you the one that saves you the most.

7. No registration, no expiry

There is nothing to register, no account to create, and no expiry date. You do not need to give us your card number online, and we do not store a copy of your card.

8. Changes to this offer

We may change or withdraw this discount in future. If we do, we will give at least 30 days' notice on this page and by signage in store, and any order placed before the change takes effect will keep the discount. We have no current intention of withdrawing it — it is one of the reasons people shop with us.

9. Privacy

If we record your card details for audit purposes, we handle them in accordance with our Privacy Policy and keep them only as long as necessary. We never record a copy of your card on this website.

Questions about this policy? Contact us on 1300 745 837 or at orders@silverlinestackle.com.au, or write to Silver Lines Tackle Co., PO Box 212, Yamba NSW 2464.
