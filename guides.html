Free fishing guides

Four guides written for people who have been fishing for decades and would rather not fight with fiddly knots, tiny print or confusing tables. No sign-up, no email required, and printable.

Free, and written by people who fish

Every guide here came out of questions customers asked us across the counter. They are checked for accuracy against the Bureau of Meteorology tide predictions and state fisheries rules, and they are written to be read once and remembered.

They are general information only and do not replace the official rules for your state or your own judgement about safety on the water. See our Website Disclaimer.

Want a printed copy? Ask at the counter or call 1300 745 837 and we will post one out free with your order.

Where we stand. Silver Lines Tackle Co. trades on Bundjalung country on the Clarence River. We pay our respects to the Bundjalung people, their Elders past and present, and acknowledge that they have cared for these waters and fisheries for tens of thousands of years. We encourage all customers to fish within state bag and size limits and to follow the rules set by your local fisheries authority.

Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST. Fishing regulations vary between states and territories — it is your responsibility to hold a current recreational fishing licence where required.
