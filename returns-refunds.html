Returns & Refunds Policy

Our 30-day change-of-mind returns policy, your Consumer Guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law, how to start a return, and how and when refunds are paid.

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Silver Lines Tackle Co. · ABN 48 271 903 556

1. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

These rights exist independently of anything in this policy, and nothing here limits them. Where this policy and the Australian Consumer Law differ, the Australian Consumer Law applies.

A product may have a “major failure” if it is unsafe, if it is significantly different from the description or sample, if it is substantially unfit for its normal purpose, or if it cannot be repaired easily and within a reasonable time. For a major failure you may choose a refund or a replacement, and you may also claim compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss.

2. Our 30-day change-of-mind returns policy

In addition to your statutory rights, we offer a 30-day change-of-mind return policy. If an item does not suit you, you may return it for a refund or exchange provided that:

  • you contact us within 30 days of receiving the goods;
  • the goods are unused and in their original condition, with all original packaging, labels, manuals and accessories;
  • you have proof of purchase — an order number, tax invoice, or a bank or card statement showing the transaction; and
  • the goods are not excluded under clause 7 below.

Under this policy we refund the price you paid for the goods. Original delivery charges are not refunded for change-of-mind returns, and you are responsible for the cost of returning the item to us, unless we agree otherwise. If we arranged the original delivery free of charge under our free-delivery threshold, we may deduct our actual outbound delivery cost from the refund — we will tell you the amount before you send the item back.

3. Faulty, damaged or incorrectly supplied goods

If goods arrive faulty, damaged in transit, or are not what you ordered, we will fix it at no cost to you. Contact us within 14 days of delivery, keep the packaging, and we will arrange one of the following at your election where the failure is major:

  • a full refund, including the delivery charge you paid;
  • a replacement of the same item; or
  • a repair, where you prefer that and it is reasonable to do so.

Where the failure is not major, we may choose to repair or replace the goods, or provide a refund if repair and replacement are not possible or are not completed within a reasonable time. We pay the return postage for faulty or incorrectly supplied goods.

4. How to start a return

  1. Contact us first. Call 1300 745 837 or email orders@silverlinestackle.com.au with your order number, the item, and the reason for the return. Please do not send goods back before telling us — we will give you a return reference and the correct return address.
  2. Pack the goods safely. Rods should be returned in a rigid tube. Include a note with your order number and return reference inside the parcel.
  3. Send it back. Post to: Returns, Silver Lines Tackle Co., PO Box 212, Yamba NSW 2464. Keep your tracking receipt. Alternatively, return the item in person to our store during opening hours.
  4. We assess and respond. We inspect returns within three business days of receiving them and will email you the outcome.

5. Refund timeframes and method

Approved refunds are processed within 5 business days of our assessment. Refunds are made to the original payment method where possible. Card refunds usually appear within 3–10 business days depending on your bank; that delay is the bank's, not ours. Bank transfer and PayPal refunds are usually same-day once processed.

We will not charge a restocking fee, and we will never require you to accept a store credit where a refund is your right under the Australian Consumer Law.

6. Exchanges

If you would prefer a different item, tell us when you contact us. If the replacement costs more, we will ask you to pay the difference before dispatch. If it costs less, we will refund the difference. Exchanges follow the same conditions as refunds.

7. Items we cannot accept back

For health, hygiene and safety reasons, and as permitted by the Australian Consumer Law, we cannot accept change-of-mind returns of:

  • inflatable life jackets and other personal flotation devices where the cylinder or inflator seal has been armed, triggered or tampered with, or where the service record card has been altered;
  • opened consumables such as bait, berley or line that has been spooled and used;
  • items that have been modified, altered, or custom-rigged at your request; and
  • gift vouchers.

This exclusion does not apply to faulty goods. If a life jacket is faulty, or does not do what it is supposed to do, your Australian Consumer Law rights apply in full and we will deal with the claim, including seeking a remedy from the manufacturer where necessary.

8. Hygiene and safety note

For everyone's safety we may ask you to describe or photograph a returned item before we accept it. This is not an attempt to refuse a valid claim; it helps us identify whether an item is faulty or has been used in a way that caused the problem, and it helps us improve the products we stock.

9. Returns of bundled or discounted items

Where items were bought as a bundle at a discount, returning part of the bundle may reduce or remove the bundle discount. We will explain the effect on your refund before you send anything back, and you can then decide whether to proceed. Returning a complete bundle attracts a full refund in accordance with this policy.

10. Complaints and escalation

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled a return, please ask to speak to the owner. If the matter remains unresolved, you may refer it to the consumer affairs agency in your state or territory (for example NSW Fair Trading or Consumer Affairs Victoria) or to the ACCC. We will cooperate fully with any regulator's investigation.

Questions about this policy? Contact us on 1300 745 837 or at orders@silverlinestackle.com.au, or write to Silver Lines Tackle Co., PO Box 212, Yamba NSW 2464.
