About Silver Lines Tackle Co.

A small Australian tackle shop that decided to specialise in the customers most shops ignore.

Twenty-five years on the same stretch of river

We opened in 1998 with one counter, a wall of hooks and a coffee tin for change. For most of that time we sold whatever the distributor pushed, like everyone else. Things changed about eight years ago when a regular — then 74 — came in, put his old rod on the counter and said he was giving it away because he could not hold it any more.

We set him up with a lighter rod, an oversized grip and a higher chair. He is 82 now, and he still fishes twice a week. He is the reason we sell what we sell, and the reason the rods on our wall are not the best-selling models in the catalogue but the easiest ones to use.

How we choose what goes on the wall

We test it ourselves first

Every rod is spooled and cast. Every chair is sat in. Every net is used to land a fish. If it is awkward for us with full strength hands, it does not go on the shelf.

We stock safety gear properly

We only sell life jackets that meet AS 4758, with the labels and service documentation intact. No grey imports, no exceptions.

We will talk you out of a sale

If the $189 combo is wrong for your jetty and the $139 one is right, we will say so. That is a shorter conversation now and a longer relationship later.

Where we stand. Silver Lines Tackle Co. trades on Bundjalung country on the Clarence River. We pay our respects to the Bundjalung people, their Elders past and present, and acknowledge that they have cared for these waters and fisheries for tens of thousands of years. We encourage all customers to fish within state bag and size limits and to follow the rules set by your local fisheries authority.

Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST. Fishing regulations vary between states and territories — it is your responsibility to hold a current recreational fishing licence where required.
