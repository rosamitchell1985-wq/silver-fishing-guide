Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, store and protect your personal information, your rights under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, and how to complain to the OAIC.

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Silver Lines Tackle Co. · ABN 48 271 903 556

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Silver Lines Tackle Co. (ABN 48 271 903 556) operates the website at silverlinestackle.com.au and a retail shop at Shop 4, 118 River Esplanade, Yamba NSW 2464. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean Silver Lines Tackle Co., and “you” means the person whose personal information we handle.

We are bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) set out in Schedule 1 to that Act. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we handle it, who we may disclose it to, and how you can access, correct or complain about it.

This policy does not apply to the practices of third parties we do not control, including payment processors and delivery carriers, although we require them to handle your information consistently with Australian privacy law.

2. What personal information we collect

We collect only the information we need to sell you gear and deliver it. Depending on how you deal with us, that may include:

  • Identity and contact details: your name, email address, phone number, and postal or delivery address.
  • Order information: what you bought, when, the amount paid, your order number and any delivery instructions you give us.
  • Payment information: the last four digits of your card, the card type and expiry month and the payment outcome. We do not store your full card number, your security code or your card PIN on this website.
  • Seniors discount verification: whether you hold an eligible Seniors Card, and in limited cases the card number where a state scheme requires us to record it for audit purposes. We never store a copy of the card itself on this website.
  • Communications: emails, letters, phone notes, and messages you send through our contact form.
  • Website usage: a small amount of technical information such as your browser type, approximate location from your IP address, and the pages you viewed, collected in a form that is not used to identify you personally.

We do not collect sensitive information as that term is defined in the Privacy Act. We do not collect information about your health, and we do not need it in order to sell you fishing gear. If you volunteer health information to us (for example, to explain why you need a particular seat height), we will treat it with extra care and will not record it on any marketing list.

3. How we collect personal information

We collect personal information directly from you in almost every case:

  • when you place an order on this website or by phone;
  • when you tick the Seniors Card box at checkout or show your card in store;
  • when you send us a message, email us or call us;
  • when you enter a competition or ask to be added to our mailing list; and
  • when you return an item or make a warranty claim.

In a small number of cases we may receive information about you from someone else — for example, if a family member places a gift order for you, or if Australia Post provides us with a delivery confirmation. We may also receive limited technical information automatically when your browser loads our pages.

If you do not give us the information we ask for, we may not be able to process your order, deliver your goods or respond to your enquiry.

4. Why we use your personal information

We use your personal information for the following purposes, each of which is a permitted purpose under the APPs:

  • To process and fulfil your order — taking payment, packing, dispatching and delivering your goods, and managing returns and warranty claims.
  • To communicate with you about your order — order confirmations, tax invoices, dispatch notices and tracking information.
  • To answer your enquiries and provide customer support, including by phone and email.
  • To apply your Seniors Card discount and verify your eligibility where we are asked to.
  • To meet our legal obligations, including taxation, consumer law and record-keeping requirements.
  • To improve our website and product range, using aggregated, de-identified information.
  • To send you marketing about products, specials and fishing information — but only if you have opted in, and only for as long as you consent. Every marketing message includes a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can also unsubscribe by phoning us.

We will not use your personal information for a purpose unrelated to those listed above without first seeking your consent, unless the law requires or permits us to do so.

5. Disclosure of your personal information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We disclose it only to the extent needed to run our business:

  • Delivery carriers such as Australia Post and their contractors, so your parcel can reach you.
  • Payment processors and our bank, to take and verify payment.
  • Our accountant, insurer and legal advisers, where required and bound by confidentiality.
  • Our website and email service providers, who host our systems under contractual privacy obligations.
  • Government agencies, regulators or courts, where we are legally compelled or where it is necessary to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

We do not disclose your personal information to overseas recipients as part of our normal operations. If that ever changes, we will tell you in this policy and take reasonable steps to ensure the overseas recipient does not breach the APPs.

6. Cookies and local storage

This website uses a small amount of browser storage to remember your cart, your text-size and contrast preferences, and your delivery details between pages. It uses no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Our Cookie Policy explains this in detail and tells you how to clear or block it.

If you block browser storage entirely, the shopping cart will not work and you will not be able to complete an order online. Please call us on 1300 745 837 and we will take your order over the phone.

7. Security of your personal information

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we hold from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Our measures include:

  • encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS) for all data submitted through this website;
  • never storing full card numbers on the website;
  • restricting access to customer records to the two staff members who need it;
  • locking and password-protecting the devices that hold customer data;
  • regular backups; and
  • securely destroying or de-identifying personal information once it is no longer needed.

No internet transmission is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme if a data breach occurs that is likely to result in serious harm.

8. How long we keep your information

We keep order records for seven years from the end of the financial year in which the order was placed. This is the period required by Australian taxation record-keeping rules, and it also covers warranty and consumer law claims. We keep marketing preferences until you withdraw your consent. Enquiries that do not lead to an order are deleted after two years. When information is no longer needed, we destroy it or de-identify it securely.

9. Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading.

Email orders@silverlinestackle.com.au or write to PO Box 212, Yamba NSW 2464. We will respond within 30 days and will provide access in the way you prefer where it is reasonably practicable to do so. We may ask you to verify your identity first, so that we do not disclose your information to someone else.

In limited circumstances we may refuse access or correction, for example where it would interfere with the privacy of another person or where the law requires us to retain records as they are. If we refuse, we will tell you why in writing and explain how you can complain.

There is normally no charge for access or correction. If a request is unusually complex we may charge the reasonable cost of retrieving and supplying the information, and we will tell you the amount before we proceed.

10. Complaints about privacy

If you think we have breached the APPs or mishandled your personal information, please contact us first:

  • Phone: 1300 745 837
  • Email: orders@silverlinestackle.com.au
  • Post: Silver Lines Tackle Co., PO Box 212, Yamba NSW 2464

We acknowledge privacy complaints within one business day and aim to resolve them within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) on 1300 363 992, by email to enquiries@oaic.gov.au, or through oaic.gov.au. The OAIC recommends that you raise the matter with us first, but you are not required to.

11. Changes to this policy

We review this policy at least annually and whenever our practices change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. If a change is significant, we will take reasonable steps to notify you, including by notice on our website or by email where we hold your address for that purpose.

Questions about this policy? Contact us on 1300 745 837 or at orders@silverlinestackle.com.au, or write to Silver Lines Tackle Co., PO Box 212, Yamba NSW 2464.
