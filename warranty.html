Warranty Policy

Warranty periods by product, what is and is not covered, how to make a claim, and how this warranty sits alongside your Australian Consumer Law guarantees.

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Silver Lines Tackle Co. · ABN 48 271 903 556

1. Warranty and your rights

The warranties described in this policy are provided by Silver Lines Tackle Co. in addition to, and not instead of, the guarantees you have under the Australian Consumer Law. Where this policy and those guarantees differ, the guarantees prevail.

You do not need to pay anything, or do anything special, to rely on your Australian Consumer Law rights. You do not need a warranty card, and you do not need to have registered the product.

3. What the warranty covers

Our warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship that appear during normal use of the product for the purpose for which it was sold. Examples include a rod guide foot lifting, a chair hinge failing under normal body weight, a reel drag losing smoothness prematurely, or a stainless fitting pitting well before its expected life.

4. What the warranty does not cover

The warranty does not cover:

  • Normal wear and tear, including worn line, faded fabric, dulled finishes and grip compression.
  • Corrosion from neglected saltwater exposure — all gear should be rinsed in fresh water after use.
  • Damage from misuse, abuse or accident, including high-sticking a rod (lifting a fish by bending the rod beyond its design angle), overloading a chair or trolley beyond its rated capacity, or dropping gear on rock or concrete.
  • Damage from using the item for a purpose it was not designed for.
  • Consumable components, including life jacket gas cylinders and water-soluble bobbins, which must be replaced at your cost. We stock replacement cylinders.
  • Alterations or repairs made by anyone other than us or an approved repairer.
  • Cosmetic damage that does not affect function.
  • Products bought second-hand or from a third party that is not an authorised retailer.

Excluding these matters from our voluntary warranty does not reduce your rights under the Australian Consumer Law where a failure is due to a defect rather than to wear, misuse or neglect.

5. How to make a warranty claim

  1. Call or email us. Phone 1300 745 837 or email orders@silverlinestackle.com.au with your order number, the product, when it was bought, and what has gone wrong.
  2. Send photographs where you can. For rods and reels, a photograph of the failure and the serial or model number speeds things up considerably.
  3. Post the item if we ask. For valid claims we pay the return postage. Send to: Warranty Claims, Silver Lines Tackle Co., PO Box 212, Yamba NSW 2464.
  4. We assess within 10 business days. For complex claims, for example where the manufacturer must be consulted, we will tell you if we need longer and keep you updated.

6. What we will do

For a valid claim we will, at your election where the failure is major, repair the item, replace it with the same or an equivalent item, or refund the price you paid. Where the failure is not major, we may choose to repair or replace the item. We aim to complete all valid claims within 21 days of assessment.

If the exact model is discontinued, we will offer an equivalent item or a refund. If you have been without usable gear for an unreasonable period because of our delay, tell us — that can turn a minor failure into a major one and change your remedies.

7. Life jacket servicing and safety equipment

Inflatable life jackets require annual servicing and you should check the cylinder and the inflator before every trip. The annual service is a safety requirement and is not a warranty expense. If your jacket fails to inflate when tested because of a manufacturing fault, that is a warranty matter and we will treat it as a serious safety issue. Under no circumstances should you rely on a life jacket whose service is overdue.

8. Statutory guarantees are not affected

This warranty is a voluntary additional benefit. It does not limit, exclude or replace the guarantees in the Australian Consumer Law. If you believe a product has a major failure, you may be entitled to a refund or replacement of your choosing, and possibly compensation for other reasonably foreseeable loss.
